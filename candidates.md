## Candidate 1 ← SELECTED

Ten thousand years I have kept this throne warm for a prophecy that keeps rescheduling. My armies stand ready. My curses are polished. Somewhere below, a hero is climbing my tower, and I find myself hoping he takes the stairs slowly. Not from fear. The torches gutter, the hall echoes, and every triumph tastes of cold iron and old smoke. When he arrives, I shall rise, I shall thunder, I shall give him the speech — the good one, with the laugh. Then, win or lose, perhaps... rest. Even darkness, hero, must eventually close its eyes.

## Candidate 2

Eight centuries I have kept the black banners flying for a prophecy with the punctuality of a tax collector. My legions polish their spears. My ravens circle. Somewhere a farm boy sharpens a sword he bought on credit, and I catch myself rehearsing mercy speeches in the mirror. Not mercy — economy. Why burn a world that is already ash and overtime? When he comes I will rise in smoke and thunder, I will say the words carved on my tomb in advance, and then, whatever happens, I intend to sit down for a very long time.

## Candidate 3

They call me the Scourge of Nine Kingdoms, but nobody warns you that scourging is mostly administration. Petitions. Grain tallies. A dragon with a union rep. Tonight the wards sing: the hero has entered the stairwell, three hundred years late and right on schedule. I have prepared the laugh, the good one, the one that makes torches shiver. After that, win or lose, I am owed a century of quiet, and I mean to collect it from whoever is left standing, starting with myself.

## Candidate 4

I had the prophecy framed. It hangs crooked; nobody dares straighten it, and I am too weary to lift the hammer. A child of light shall end my reign — splendid, fine, I have sent the child tutors, maps, a decent sword. Still he dawdles in some village, ripening. Meanwhile I must keep being dreadful on schedule: the raids, the omens, the laugh at executions. Do you know how long a laugh must echo to sound eternal? Longer than I have breath for. Come quickly, child. The dark is holding this pose, and its arms are shaking.

(524 characters)

## Candidate 5

Conquering the world was the easy half. Ruling it is a thousand small hours, each heavier than a siege. Petitions from goblins. Grain reports from provinces I burned on principle and now must feed. My crown fits; that is the worst of it — I have grown into the shape of this exhaustion. Tonight the bells say the hero is close. I should feel the old hunger. Instead I feel something almost like gratitude. Whatever he brings — death, defeat, a throne I can finally stand up from — he brings an ending. I have not had one of those in centuries.

(543 characters)

## Candidate 6

My necromancers unionized last spring. My dragon takes naps I am not permitted to interrupt. The moat needs dredging, the curse on the eastern gate needs renewing, and the mirror that shows me my enemies mostly shows me my own face, older. I began this reign with fire in my chest. Now there is only the coal of it, banked and grey, and I tend it out of habit, the way one tends a grave. When the hero comes I will stand. I will be magnificent. It is the last item on today's list, and lists, at least, end.

## Candidate 7

Another dawn, another ambush to schedule. Do you know what evil requires, hero? Paperwork. Supply lines. A dungeon staffed through winter. I conquered nine kingdoms before my knees began their own rebellion, and now I plan campaigns around the hours the pain sleeps. It never sleeps. Neither, apparently, do I. When I was young I wanted eternity. Nobody warns you eternity is mostly meetings. So come, then. Raise your shining sword. One of us leaves this hall free of all of it, and for the first time in a thousand years I cannot decide which of us I am rooting for.

(568 characters)

## Candidate 8

Eight hundred years I have practiced this moment in the mirror: the rising, the shadow, the voice like a closing gate. The mirror is patient. I am not, anymore. My potions master brews me something for the dreams — the dreams are all of ledgers. Armies I raised now pension off; monsters I made now write memoirs. Everything I built outlives my strength to hold it, which I suppose is what building means. So let the boy climb. Let the torches flare. I will give him the performance of the age. Then the Dark Lord, at long last, will sit down.

(543 characters)
